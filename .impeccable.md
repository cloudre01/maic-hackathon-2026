## Design Context

### Users
Malaysian lender analysts assessing microbusinesses and workers with irregular income. They need to inspect economic evidence, compare financing capacity and understand the limits of a prototype.

### Brand Personality
Calm, credible, humane. Working name: Arus (flow). Clear evidence takes precedence over an impressive-looking score.

### Aesthetic Direction
User confirmed calm ivory and green. Editorial typography, generous space, forest-green navigation and deliberate ledger-like data tables. Light mode, accessible contrast, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support.

### Design Principles
- Separate measured model evidence from simulated Malaysian assessments.
- Explain an assessment beside the numbers that produced it.
- Make uncertainty visible without overwhelming the workflow.
- Use forms and tables with explicit labels and keyboard focus.
- Show useful cash-flow charts with axes and actual values.
